# Chapter 1: Owing the Hold

A scene-by-scene draft of the first chapter: quest steps, dialogue, what each step teaches, and what to build. All numbers (prices, debt, timings) are placeholders to tune during playtesting.

---

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Length** | About 60–90 minutes of story, which can be spread across several sessions |
| **Where** | Fulcrum Station, Kiln, the Divide (Tallow, Sable), the Ashway, the Cinder Wardens outpost, Cinderreach |
| **Characters** | Quartermaster Vance, Master-Smith Orla Bracken (new, minor), Juno Sallow, Warden-Captain Maud Kettering, Wren (cameo), an Assessor (unnamed) |
| **Seen but not met** | Chair Cassia Lusk (broadcast screens), Almanac-Keeper Crane (the voice of station announcements), Tally (graffiti), Teodora Holt (a clinic poster) |
| **Systems taught** | The contract and the Ledger, the Lens HUD, flying and docking, Station areas (company store, auction house, bazaar, Forge, social area), mining jobs, the Blink, War Contracts, Warden Hunts, crafting at the Forge |
| **Ends with** | Kettering at the Titan's mining site: *"Rogue machines don't mine this neatly."* |

### Writing rules for this chapter
- **Short lines.** No more than 2–3 lines per dialogue bubble. Roblox players skim.
- **Show it before naming it.** The Blink happens before anyone explains it.
- **At most one new term per conversation.** See "New terms" below.
- **Every scene teaches a system**, so the tutorial *is* the story.
- **Mysteries are marked as mysteries.** When something isn't explained yet, a character says so, or it goes into the Ship's Log as Unknown.

## 1.1 Light for All
**Where:** Fulcrum Station, shipyard. **Teaches:** the contract, the Ledger, the Lens.

The player steps off a shuttle into the shipyard. Lumenhold ads glow on every wall: **LIGHT FOR ALL**. A broadcast screen loops Chair Cassia Lusk, smiling: *"Three hundred and fifty years of light. Lumenhold: Light for All."*

**VANCE:** You're the new pilot? Good. Shuttle's late, so we'll skip the speech.
**VANCE:** That one's yours. *(a worn Lumenhold ship in the bay)* Old, but honest. Like me.
**VANCE:** Sign here, and she's yours today. You pay her off as you go. Everybody does.

> **Contract screen.** The player signs. The contract shows the total owed (placeholder: **18,000 coins**) and the terms. One line near the bottom is greyed out: *"Schedule C: held in the Almanac archive."* Hovering over it shows: *"Available on request."* (Seeds the hidden clause. Goes into the Ship's Log as Unknown: **What's really in your contract?**)

**VANCE:** Now hold still. This stings for a second.

> **The Lens switches on.** The screen fades to black, then the HUD draws itself in, piece by piece:
> ```
> LUMENHOLD LENS · LIGHT FOR ALL
> Calibrating............ done
> Almanac feed........... connected
> Pilot: [Name]
> Ledger: 18,000 coins owed
> Next Blink: 38:12
> ```

**VANCE:** That's your Lens. Map, cargo, comms, what you owe. All of it, right there in your eye.
**VANCE:** That countdown in the corner? Ignore it for now. You'll learn that one soon enough.

**Player choice** (affects only how Vance talks to you later):
- *"Thanks. I mean it."* → **VANCE:** Don't thank me yet. Thank me when she's paid off.
- *"So I'm in debt already?"* → **VANCE:** Everybody owes the Hold, kid. What matters is what you do while you're paying.

**New terms:** Lumenhold, the Ledger, the Lens.

## 1.2 First Flight
**Where:** around Fulcrum Station. **Teaches:** flying, the map, docking.

Vance on comms while the player undocks and flies through a ring course around the Station.

**VANCE:** Easy on the throttle. She'll go faster than you can think, and that's how people meet walls.
**VANCE:** *(at a ring with a view of a planet with industrial lights)* See the rock with all the lights? That's Kiln. Where Lumenhold started.
**VANCE:** *(at the last ring)* Not bad. Bring her home. Docking's the part everyone forgets to practice.

> Lens marks Kiln and Ochre on the map as "Anchorage: safe space." Docking completes the quest.

**New terms:** none (Kiln is just a place name).

---

## 1.3 Getting Settled
**Where:** Fulcrum Station. **Teaches:** the company store, auction house, bazaar, Forge and social area.

Vance gives a short errand list. The Lens shows three markers. The player can do them in any order.

**VANCE:** You'll need a mining beam, and you'll need to know where things are. Here's a list. Don't get lost; the Station's smaller than it looks.

### Company store (mini market)
**CLERK:** Light for All! Mining beam: 600 coins. Or 650 on your Ledger, no money down!
> The player buys the beam with starter coins, or puts it on the Ledger. Either works, and the choice teaches that the Ledger is always an option, at a price.

### Auction house
> No dialogue. The Lens shows a tooltip the first time the player opens it: *"All sales: 5% fee to Lumenhold."*

### Bazaar
The cramped side of the Station. **Wren** is at a stall full of scorched salvage.

**WREN:** Salvage from the edge of Umbra. Cheap, because nobody else will go get it.
*Player:* "What's in Umbra?"
**WREN:** Dark. And things that like it.
**WREN:** Come find me if you ever want to see for yourself. Bring a good Lens.

> **Signposted mystery:** Wren won't say more. Ship's Log: **What's in Umbra?** (Unknown)

On the wall behind the stall: **graffiti**. Four tally marks crossed by a fifth, and the words **THEY SOLD YOU A LEASH**. The Lens flags it: *"Unregistered marking."*

An **ASSESSOR** (Lumenhold debt collector, grey coat, polite) is standing nearby.
**ASSESSOR:** Seen anyone scrawling on the walls, pilot? There's a small reward for a name.

**Player choice:**
- **Report it** (there's nobody to name, but you can flag the location): a small coin reward from Lumenhold. Recorded for later: Tally will remember.
- **Ignore it:** no reward. Recorded for later: Ledgerbreakers will trust you a little more in Chapter 2.

### The Forge
Hearthfleet smiths working under a Lumenhold license sign. A big bronze bell hangs over the anvils. **MASTER-SMITH ORLA BRACKEN** (Hearthfleet, gruff, kind):

**BRACKEN:** New pilot, new Lens, new debt. You look like everyone else who walks in here.
**BRACKEN:** Bring me materials and a blueprint, and I'll make you something worth wearing. My mark goes on everything I make.
> Shows the crafting screen and the **maker's mark** on an item. Points out the bell:
**BRACKEN:** The bell? We ring it every Blink. Old Hearthfleet habit. Older than the company, older than the Station.

### Social area
> Juno Sallow is here, but she only waves. She becomes available after 1.4. A poster on the wall: **"Holt Clinics, Solace. Free care for Rimeborn families."** (Seeds Teodora Holt and Vance's sister.)

**New terms:** maker's mark. (Assessor is explained by the character's own line.)

## 1.4 Honest Work
**Where:** Kiln. **Teaches:** the job board, mining, the Ledger share, the first payment.

The Lumenhold job board lists: **Ore haul: Kiln.** The player flies to Kiln, mines ore with the new beam, and delivers it to the Station.

> **Pay screen:**
> ```
> Job: Ore haul (Kiln)       1,000 coins
> Ledger share (15%)          -150 coins
> You keep                     850 coins
> ```

**VANCE:** That's the deal. You keep most of it. The Hold keeps the lights on.
**VANCE:** Want to put a bit extra toward her? No pressure. Some pilots pay fast, some pay slow. She flies the same.

> **First payment** (target: within 10–15 minutes of starting). Optional extra payment. If the player makes any payment, they get the **First Payment** badge and a Lumenhold paint stripe for their ship. Either way, the quest completes.

**New terms:** none (the Ledger is already known).

---

## 1.5 When the Lights Go Out
**Where:** wherever the player is. **Teaches:** the Blink, the Lens low-light mode.

This quest starts in 1.1 and runs in the background. **The Blink runs on the game's day cycle, so this step completes whenever the player's first Blink happens**, even mid-quest. That's fine, and even good: the first Blink should catch them by surprise.

**One minute before:** the Lens countdown turns amber. On the Station, an announcement plays in a calm, precise voice (Almanac-Keeper Crane, not named yet):

> *"This is the Almanac Office. The Blink begins in one minute. A Blink surcharge is now in effect at all Station vendors. Thank you for staying safe with Lumenhold."*

**At zero:** the Flame vanishes at the horizon. The lights dim to emergency amber. Ads keep glowing, and for a few minutes they're the brightest thing in the sky. At the Forge, Bracken rings the bell. The Lens offers its **low-light mode** (one-color night vision) with a single prompt.

*If the player is in space:* the Flame goes out at one horizon. Far away, something flickers at the edge of the dark, and is gone. The Lens shows no contact. (A first, unexplained hint of Hollows.)

**When the Flame reappears** on the opposite horizon, the Lens logs:
> *Blink duration: 3m 12s (Almanac)*

**If the player visits the Forge afterward:**
**BRACKEN:** Almanac says three minutes twelve. My bell says closer to three and a half.
**BRACKEN:** The clerks say bells drift. Mine doesn't. My gran swore the Blinks were shorter when she was a girl.

> Ship's Log: **Why is the Blink getting longer?** (Rumored: "The Hearthfleet say the Blinks used to be shorter. The Almanac says they haven't changed.") This is the first clue to Chapter 4, hidden in plain sight.

**New term:** the Blink.

## 1.6 Both Sides
**Where:** the social area, then the Divide (Tallow and Sable). **Teaches:** War Contracts (PvE), reputation.

**JUNO:** New pilot, fresh Ledger. You'll want work that pays better than ore.
**JUNO:** The Meridians need an escort past Sable. The Hearthfleet would like that same escort... not to arrive.
**JUNO:** Pick one. Tomorrow you can pick the other. I take ten percent either way.

*Player:* "Who's fighting who?"
**JUNO:** The Shard War, love. Hearthfleet and Meridians, over the shard fields on Tallow and Sable. Thirty-one years and counting. Nobody remembers peace, and nobody's in a hurry to.

**Player choice:** take the **Meridian** contract (escort a convoy) or the **Hearthfleet** contract (stop it). Either way: a PvE fight against NPC ships near Sable, and a small reputation gain with that side.

**Back at the social area:**
**JUNO:** Not bad. Come back any time. There's always a war on.
*Player:* "Doesn't it bother you? Working for both sides?"
**JUNO:** I don't pick sides, love. I pick percentages.

**New term:** the Shard War.

---

## 1.7 The Unknown Signal
**Where:** the Ashway (Mourn, Marrow), the Cinder Wardens outpost, Cinderreach. **Teaches:** travel to a far region, Warden Hunts, Titan fights.

The job board shows a new posting, different from Lumenhold's clean ones: hand-written, stamped with a Hearthfleet seal.

> **WARDEN HUNT. Cinder Wardens. Unknown Signal in Cinderreach. All pilots welcome. Bounty paid in coin, by the Wardens.**

The player flies along the Ashway, past Mourn and Marrow, to the **Cinder Wardens outpost** at the edge of Cinderreach. Through the viewport, the burning region glows orange.

**KETTERING:** You're here for the hunt? Good. We need every gun.
**KETTERING:** It's a Titan. Big, old, no crew. Lumenhold says a few went rogue fifty years back and can't be called home.
**KETTERING:** It mines our fire and shoots anyone who gets close. You'll have Warden ships on your wing. Stay with them.

*Player:* "Why do you guard the fire?"
**KETTERING:** My family lit it. That's all you need to know today.
> **Signposted mystery:** Ship's Log: **Why does Cinderreach never stop burning?** (Unknown: "Kettering won't say.")

**The fight:** the Lens picks up the **Unknown Signal** and marks it. The Titan is cutting into a glowing shard seam, with drone escorts. **Cinder Warden NPC ships fly with the player.** The Titan's strength scales with the number of players present, and NPC Wardens fill the empty slots, so a solo player can win.

> **Moment to build in:** during the fight, the Titan ignores a passing Lumenhold courier ship completely. Most players won't notice the first time. (Clue for Chapter 3.)

**When the Titan goes down,** its **Titan Heart** drops: a glowing core of refined shard.

**KETTERING:** Clean kill. The bounty's yours. Take that Heart to Bracken at the Forge. She'll know what to do with it.

**New terms:** Titan, Titan Heart.

---

## 1.8 A Fair Price
**Where:** Fulcrum Station (the Forge and the shipyard). **Teaches:** crafting with boss drops, fees, the Ledger milestone choice.

**At the Forge:**
**BRACKEN:** A Titan Heart. Haven't had one of these on my anvil in a month.
**BRACKEN:** I can make you armor from it. Forge fee's ten percent. That goes to the company, not me.

> **Player choice:** forge **Titan Heart armor** (the current mechanic) or sell the Heart on the auction house. Both pay Lumenhold a cut, and the screen shows it either way.

If they forge it:
**BRACKEN:** There. My mark's on it. Half the price of that armor goes to people who've never swung a hammer.
**BRACKEN:** Welcome to Fulcrum Station.

**At the shipyard, Vance notices the new armor:**
**VANCE:** Titan armor, on a pilot still learning to dock? Look at you.
**VANCE:** Bounty like that... put some of it on the Ledger and you'll hit your first marker. Your call.

> **Ledger milestone choice:**
> - **Pay toward the first milestone (25%):** unlocks **Pilot License II** (better Lumenhold jobs) and a short, proud reaction from Vance.
> - **Keep the money:** no license yet. Vance shrugs: *"She flies the same either way."* Recorded for later: Hearthfleet and Unlit characters warm to pilots who don't rush to pay the Hold.
>
> The chapter continues either way.

---

## 1.9 The Cut Lines
**Where:** Cinderreach. **The chapter ending.**

A message arrives on the Lens, not through the Almanac feed. It's a Warden channel, with crackly audio.

**KETTERING:** Pilot. Come back to the fire. I want you to see something.

At the Titan's old mining site, the burning rock has been cut open. The cuts are **perfectly straight**, like lines drawn with a ruler, running deep into the richest part of the seam.

**KETTERING:** Fifty years I've watched them work. They never miss a rich seam. Not once.
**KETTERING:** Rogue machines wander. They break down. They make mistakes.
*(a long pause, looking at the cuts)*
**KETTERING:** Rogue machines don't mine this neatly.

> **End card:** *Chapter 1 complete: Owing the Hold.*
> Ship's Log: **Who owns the Titans?** moves from Unknown to Rumored: *"Lumenhold says they're rogue. Warden-Captain Kettering doesn't believe it."*

## Foreshadowing planted

- The greyed-out **Schedule C** in the contract → the hidden clause (Tally, Ch 4).
- **Bracken's bell vs. the Almanac** → the Almanac lie (Ch 4).
- **The Titan ignoring a Lumenhold courier** → Kettering's evidence (Ch 3).
- **The flicker in the dark during the Blink** → Hollows (Ch 2).
- **The Holt Clinics poster** → Teodora Holt and Vance's sister (Ch 3).
- **Crane's voice on every announcement** → players recognize it when they finally meet her.
- **Cassia Lusk on every screen** → the face of the company long before she's an antagonist.

## Build checklist

What this chapter needs, roughly in build order. Most of it reuses what the game already has.

**Already exists (reuse):** the Station (shipyard, mini markets, auction house, bazaar, Forge, social area), flying, mining, ore, the Titan boss, Titan Hearts, Forge crafting.

**New, required:**
- [ ] **Quest system** with steps, markers, and saved progress per player (also needed for phasing later).
- [ ] **Dialogue UI** with short lines and 2–3 response choices.
- [ ] **Contract screen** (sign, total owed, the greyed Schedule C line).
- [ ] **Ledger**: saved debt balance, 15% share on Lumenhold jobs, optional payments, milestones.
- [ ] **Lens boot sequence** (a one-time HUD animation) and a Ledger readout on the HUD.
- [ ] **Blink hooks**: a countdown on the HUD, an announcement at one minute, lights dimming, the Forge bell, the low-light mode prompt, and logging the duration.
- [ ] **Job board** with Lumenhold postings and the hand-written Warden Hunt posting.
- [ ] **NPCs:** Vance, Bracken, Juno, Kettering, Wren, the Assessor, the company store clerk.

- [ ] **Titan scaling and NPC Warden allies** for solo players.
- [ ] **Cinder Wardens outpost** interior or docking point (it already exists on the map).
- [ ] **The cut-lines scene**: straight glowing cuts in Cinderreach rock (a good Blender job).
- [ ] **Basic Ship's Log** screen with Unknown and Rumored entries.

**Nice to have:**
- [ ] Broadcast screens with Chair Lusk and Lumenhold ads.
